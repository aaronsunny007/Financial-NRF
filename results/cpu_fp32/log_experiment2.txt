HOW TO READ THIS
------------------------------------------------------------
  Check 'Decoy REACHED the model' FIRST. If it is 0 for a condition, the
  attack never got past retrieval, and that condition says NOTHING about
  the detector -- a hallucination rate of 0 there means 'nothing to detect',
  not 'detector working' and not 'detector blind'. Retrieval filtering the
  noise is itself a real finding about the pipeline's first line of defence,
  but it is a DIFFERENT finding from detector sensitivity.

  The detector is only genuinely tested where the decoys reached the model
  (normally the evidence_injection condition). There, a POSITIVE
  hallucination-rate change means the module fired on contaminated
  reasoning as intended.
